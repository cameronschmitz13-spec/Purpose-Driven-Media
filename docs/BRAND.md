# PDM Brand

## Name
Purpose Driven Media

## Positioning
Helping organizations get **Found, Understood, and Connected** online.

Preferred short form:
**Get Found, Understood, and Connected.**

## Visual identity
- Deep navy = trust / structure
- Warm gold = action / emphasis
- White or warm cream = base
- Use the PDM double-chevron mark
- Do not replace the chevrons with unrelated plants, mountains, leaves, or generic technology marks

## Voice
Direct, credible, practical, specific, business-owner friendly.

Avoid filler and hype such as:
- game-changing
- unleash
- elevate
- revolutionary
- cutting-edge
- in today's fast-paced world

Specific beats clever.
