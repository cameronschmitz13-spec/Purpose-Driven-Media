# CRO Baseline

## Main conversion blocker to avoid
Do not make visitors translate broad agency language into a concrete business problem.

## Recommended hero direction

Eyebrow:
**PDM Visibility Screening**

H1:
**Can Customers Actually Find Your Business Online?**

Support:
**Purpose Driven Media identifies where your business or organization is easy to find, where people may get confused, and what to fix first.**

Primary CTA:
**Start My Visibility Screening**

Secondary CTA:
**See a Sample Report**

## Strong alternate direct-response hook
**People Can't Choose a Business They Can't Find.**

Support:
**See how visible your organization really is—and get a clear roadmap showing what to improve first.**

## Homepage order
1. Problem + CTA
2. Sample Visibility Screening
3. What PDM checks
4. What the visitor receives
5. Real project/client proof
6. How the process works
7. Relevant services/software
8. FAQ
9. Final CTA

## Principle
The diagnostic sells the treatment.
