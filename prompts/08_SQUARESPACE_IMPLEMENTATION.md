## Objective
Implement the approved CRO, UX, trust, and on-page SEO improvements directly in the existing Squarespace site without rebuilding it from scratch.

## Before editing
1. Inspect the live homepage and current Squarespace page structure.
2. Identify existing sections that can be reused.
3. Identify what already matches the approved PDM direction.
4. List the smallest set of high-impact changes.
5. Preserve working forms, links, navigation, analytics, and integrations.

## Implementation order
### 1. Hero
Use the approved conversion direction:
- Eyebrow: `PDM Visibility Screening`
- H1 direction: `Can Customers Actually Find Your Business Online?`
- Supporting copy should explain that PDM identifies where an organization is easy to find, where people may get confused, and what to fix first.
- Primary CTA: `Start My Visibility Screening`
- Secondary CTA: `See a Sample Report`

Adapt wording only when needed to fit the existing layout without weakening clarity.

### 2. Sample Visibility Screening
Place a clear sample-report section high on the homepage.
Requirements:
- clearly fictional organization
- clear "Sample Report — fictional organization for illustration only" disclosure
- legible on mobile
- explain what PDM actually checks
- CTA after the sample: `What would your organization score?` → `Start My Visibility Screening`

### 3. Flow
Aim for:
1. Problem + CTA
2. Sample Visibility Screening
3. What PDM checks
4. What the visitor receives
5. Real project/client proof
6. How the process works
7. Relevant services/software
8. FAQ
9. Final CTA

Do not force sections that lack truthful content. Use TODOs instead of fabricated proof.

### 4. Trust
Use only real proof already available.
Do not invent testimonials, results, ratings, client counts, or software capabilities.

### 5. Mobile
Check common phone widths:
- first viewport clarity
- readable type
- CTA visibility
- tap targets
- sample-report legibility
- excessive spacing
- navigation
- section stacking

### 6. On-page SEO
Where Squarespace allows:
- set one meaningful H1
- use intent-aligned page title and meta description
- confirm clean canonical behavior
- confirm social sharing title/description/image
- check image alt text
- ensure headings are logically nested
- preserve indexing
- do not add thin keyword-stuffed copy

Recommended homepage SEO direction:
Title: `Online Visibility Screening for Businesses & Organizations | Purpose Driven Media`
Meta: `Find out how easily customers can discover, understand, and connect with your organization online. Get a practical Visibility Screening and prioritized roadmap.`

## Publish discipline
Before publishing:
- preview desktop

- verify forms
- verify navigation
- check for broken/missing images
- check for accidental duplicate H1s
- check spelling
- confirm fictional sample is labeled
- confirm no fabricated proof
