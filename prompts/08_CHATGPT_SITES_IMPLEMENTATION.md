## Implementation order

### 1. Hero
Preferred direction:
- Eyebrow: `PDM Visibility Screening`
- H1: `Can Customers Actually Find Your Business Online?`
- Support: explain that PDM identifies where a business or organization is easy to find, where people may get confused, and what to fix first.
- Primary CTA: `Start My Visibility Screening`
- Secondary CTA: `See a Sample Report`

Adapt wording only when necessary to fit the existing design without weakening clarity.

### 2. Sample Visibility Screening
Place a clear sample-report section high on the homepage.

Requirements:
- fictional organization only
- visible disclosure: `Sample Report — fictional organization for illustration only.`
- readable on mobile
- explain what PDM actually checks
- CTA after sample:
  `What would your organization score?`
  `Start My Visibility Screening`

### 3. Flow
Target sequence:
1. Problem + CTA
2. Sample Visibility Screening
3. What PDM checks
4. What the visitor receives
5. Real proof
6. How the process works
7. Relevant services/software
8. FAQ
9. Final CTA

Do not force content that is not supported by real facts.

### 4. Trust

- testimonials
- metrics
- ratings
- client counts
- outcomes
- product capabilities
